# AI director

The Director panel runs an agent CLI you already have installed and signed in to. JoeFlux never handles your provider credentials; each CLI uses its own sign-in.

## Start with the brief

Fill in the **quick brief**: audience, main message, visible evidence, format, duration, tone, call to action and brand. The director reads it with `brief_get` at the start of every turn and does not ask again about settled choices. **Grill this concept** starts an optional deep interview that asks only the questions whose prerequisites are already answered.

## Ask for outcomes

> "Build a short product walkthrough: introduce the feature, show the important action, hold on the result, then close with a next step."

> "Skip the loading wait, hold the completed result for two seconds, and preview the transition into the next shot."

> "Suggest better animation for this shot."

For animation requests the director reads the `animation-engines` skill, offers two or three engine options with a recommendation, and waits for you to choose before changing anything.

## What the director can and cannot do

- Every change goes through a typed tool, appears as a card in the chat and can be undone from the card or the top bar.
- After a visual change it calls `quality_review` and looks at three rendered frames itself. Its heuristics are not approval; you decide when a shot is right.
- Tools never modify source recordings, and projects stay silent. Claude Code runs are limited to the JoeFlux tools; other CLIs keep their own permission settings, so use agents only with projects you trust.

See [Agents and MCP](../architecture/agents.md) for how runs are isolated and [Token efficiency](../architecture/token-efficiency.mdx) for what each turn costs.
