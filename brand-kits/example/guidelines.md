# Example Studio brand guidelines

- Voice: plain, confident sentences in sentence case. One idea per shot.
- Logo: use the on-dark logo over dark themes and backgrounds, the on-light logo over light ones. Keep it in the top-left corner at its configured size. Never stretch, recolor or rotate it.
- Color: violet `#8B7CFF` is the accent for focus and calls to action. Teal `#3DD6C6` is a secondary highlight; use it sparingly.
- Motion: restrained entrances, readable holds of at least two seconds, no decorative loops.
- Bookends: open with the intro clip and close with the outro clip when the video is published externally.
